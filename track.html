The problem

Workers wait for instructions

What Track does

Built for tool rooms.

Projects with steps and dates

Start a project from a template, get every step with a date in working days. Change one date and the rest re-plan.

My work, per person

Each worker opens one page on their phone and sees what is theirs today, marks it done, adds a remark or a photo.

A progress link for the customer

Share a link. The customer sees where their die is and what comes next, without a login and without calling you.

Reminders that reach people

Telegram, email and in-app reminders for steps that are due or late, with quiet hours so nobody is pinged at night.

Reports and photos on each step

Trial reports, inspection photos and customer files live on the step they belong to, not in a WhatsApp group.

Marathi and English

Every screen and every message in the language your team reads. The owner can work in English, the floor in Marathi.

FAQ

No. They open a link you share and see the progress of their project. You can revoke the link at any time.

Yes. The owner can export every project and every uploaded report from Settings at any time.
