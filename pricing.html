Billing questions

Before you decide.

Are the prices inclusive of GST?

No. Every price on this page is plus GST. You receive a tax invoice with our GSTIN for every payment, and you can claim the input credit.

What if an instalment is late?

We remind you before the due date. Access continues as long as instalments are paid; if one stays unpaid after reminders, the account becomes read-only until it is settled. Nothing is deleted.

What happens at renewal?

The yearly subscription renews at the then-current price. The onboarding fee is paid once and never again.

Can I pay monthly instead?

Our plans are yearly so that your factory is never interrupted mid-season. If a yearly payment is difficult, talk to us on WhatsApp and we will find a way.

Do I need both products?

No. P2 is for factories that buy material and dispatch goods. Track is for tool rooms and die makers who run projects. Some companies use both; most use one.
