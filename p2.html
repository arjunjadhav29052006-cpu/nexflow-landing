What P2 does

Built for factory floors.

Not a generic inventory app. Every feature exists because a MIDC factory needed it.

GRN, goods receipt

Log every delivery: supplier, invoice number, rate, quantity, GST rate. Stock updates instantly, and a repeated supplier invoice gets a warning before it is saved.

Dispatch and challans

Create a dispatch, print or WhatsApp the challan. It carries a QR code the customer scans to confirm delivery. Raw-material dispatches and job-work movements are covered too.

Products, BOM and production

Define finished products with a bill of materials. Issue material to production, see work in progress, and let a dispatch deduct the right raw material automatically.

Invoices and payments

Tax invoices from confirmed dispatches, or one consolidated invoice for the month. Email them to the customer, record receipts, see who is overdue.

Export for your accountant

Tally Prime and Zoho files, a GSTR-1 workbook with the HSN table and the challan table, an HSN audit and the 43B(h) supplier-payment report. Your CA files from these. Nexflow itself never files a return.

GSTR-2B reconciliation Pro

Upload the portal's GSTR-2B file. Nexflow matches it against every GRN and shows which supplier invoices are missing, so your CA knows whom to chase before the 20th.

Telegram alerts

Every morning at 8 AM the owner gets a briefing: what is below reorder level, what came in, what went out. Instant alerts when something needs attention.

AI copilot in Marathi Pro

Staff logins and roles Pro

Owner, supervisor, storekeeper, accountant. Each person sees what their job needs. When someone leaves, their replacement is live the same day.

FAQ

Common questions.

How long does setup take?

Most factories are live within a week of sharing their material and supplier lists. We agree the go-live date with you before you pay, and we do the loading and training ourselves.

Does it work without internet?

It needs internet, like WhatsApp does. A 4G phone is enough. There is nothing to install and nothing to back up on your side.

Is my data safe? Who can see it?

Each company's data is isolated by database access rules, encrypted in transit and at rest, and backed up daily. You can export everything any time. Nexflow staff look at your data only to help you, when you ask.

What if I want to leave or move to other software?

Export everything in one click: every table as a spreadsheet, a Tally file and every invoice and challan as PDF. Your data was always yours.

Does Nexflow file my GST returns?

No. Nexflow prepares the invoices and the exports your CA needs: the GSTR-1 workbook, the Tally file, the HSN audit and the GSTR-2B reconciliation. Filing stays with your CA, as it should.

What is the AI copilot? Do I need to know how to use AI?

No. You type in Marathi, Hindi or English the way you would on WhatsApp, and it answers from your own stock and purchase data. It is read-only: it never changes a record on its own. It is part of the Pro plan, with a daily limit of questions.

My storekeeper is not comfortable with computers.

If he can use WhatsApp, he can use P2. The GRN form is three fields, in Marathi. We train him at the factory and the videos stay available.

Why does GSTR-2B reconciliation matter?

Your input credit depends on your suppliers filing their returns. If one has not, that credit is not available to you even though you paid the GST. Reconciliation shows exactly which supplier invoice is missing, so your CA can chase it before the filing date instead of discovering it afterwards.

We do job work for a larger company. Does P2 handle that?

Yes. The principal's material is tracked separately from your own stock, challans carry the right movement purpose, the job-work register watches return deadlines, and your CA gets an ITC-04 working paper.
