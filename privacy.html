Privacy policy

How Nexflow handles your data

Last updated 22 October 2026

This page is in English. The English text is the one that applies.

Who operates the service

Nexflow P2 and Nexflow Track are operated by Nexflow Automations, Umbraj, Karad, Satara district, Maharashtra, India ("Nexflow", "we"). Contact: hello@nexflowautomations.in or WhatsApp +91 72489 32468.

This policy covers the two web applications (nexflowautomations.in and track.nexflowautomations.in) and this website (www.nexflowautomations.in).

What we collect

  • Account details of the people a subscribing company adds as users: name, email address, phone number, and role. If you sign in with Google, the name and email address of your Google profile.
  • Business data the company enters: materials, suppliers, customers, products, goods receipts, dispatches, challans, invoices, payments, projects, steps, remarks, and the files and photos uploaded to them.
  • Messages to the AI copilot in Nexflow P2, and photos sent to it, so that it can answer from your company's data.
  • Technical records: sign-in times, the device and browser used, and error logs, kept to keep the service secure and working.
  • This website stores only your language choice in your browser. It sets no advertising cookies and uses no analytics trackers.

How we use it

Only to run the service for the subscribing company: showing its data to its own users, producing challans, invoices, reports and exports, sending the alerts and reminders the company has switched on, answering copilot questions, and providing support when the company asks for it. We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it. We do not use your business data to train AI models.

Who can see your data

Each company's data is isolated by database access rules: a user sees only the company they belong to, and only what their role allows. Nexflow staff look at a company's data only to help that company, when it asks, for example during setup or support. Where a job-work principal and its vendor both use Nexflow P2, the principal sees only the material and job-charge invoices that belong to it, through access rules built for that purpose.

Where it is stored and who processes it

Data is stored on Supabase in the Mumbai (India) region, encrypted in transit and at rest, with daily backups. We share data only with the sub-processors needed to deliver the service, and each receives only what its part requires:

  • Supabase: database, authentication, file storage and server functions.
  • Vercel: hosting of the web applications and this website.
  • Resend: delivery of emails such as invoices, invitations and reminders.
  • Telegram: alerts and reminders to users who connect their Telegram account.
  • Anthropic: the AI model behind the Nexflow P2 copilot and GRN photo reading. It receives the question or photo and the data needed to answer it.
  • Google: sign-in, where a company chooses to use it.

How long we keep it

For as long as the company's subscription is active and for 90 days after it ends, so that the company can export its data or return. After that the data is deleted from the live database; backups expire on their own schedule. The company can ask for earlier deletion at any time.

Your choices

  • Export: the owner of a subscribing company can export all of its data at any time from the application (every table as a spreadsheet, a Tally file and every invoice and challan as PDF in Nexflow P2; every project and uploaded report in Nexflow Track).
  • Correction: users can correct their own account details in the application; business records are corrected by the company's own users.
  • Deletion: to delete a user or the whole company's data, email hello@nexflowautomations.in from the company's registered address. We confirm deletion by email.

Children

The services are for businesses and are not directed at anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated and subscribing companies are informed by email before the change takes effect.

Contact
